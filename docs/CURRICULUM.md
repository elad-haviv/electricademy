# Curriculum Map

The full topic tree, from zero to advanced. Each topic shows which audiences it serves:
**B** = Basics, **G** = Bagrut, **T** = Technician/Practical Engineer (MAHAT), **A** = Academic, **L** = Licensing, **P** = Professionals.

> The mapping to official syllabi (Ministry of Education, MAHAT, licensing) has to be checked against the current official program documents before content is marked "aligned". Keep that mapping in each lesson's frontmatter once it is verified.

## 1. Foundations: B, G, L
- Charge, voltage, current, resistance; units and prefixes
- Ohm's law; power and energy; kWh and the electricity bill
- Series, parallel and mixed circuits; voltage and current dividers
- Conductors and insulators; resistivity; temperature effects
- Voltage drop in cables
- Measurement: multimeter, clamp meter, insulation tester (concepts)
- Safety basics: electric shock and its effects on the body

## 2. DC circuit analysis: G, T, A
- Kirchhoff's laws; node and mesh analysis
- Thevenin, Norton and superposition
- Capacitors and inductors; RC and RL transients

## 3. AC fundamentals: G, T, A, L
- Sine waves, RMS, phase and phasors
- Impedance; RLC circuits; resonance
- Active, reactive and apparent power; power factor and its correction
- Three-phase systems: star and delta, line and phase quantities, balanced and unbalanced loads
- Harmonics (intro)

## 4. Electrical machines: T, A, P
- Magnetic circuits
- Transformers: equivalent circuit, losses, efficiency, connection groups, parallel operation
- Induction motors: principle, torque-speed curve, starting methods (DOL, star-delta, soft starter, VFD)
- Synchronous machines and generators: excitation, AVR, synchronization ⟶ *genset-edu*
- DC machines (overview)

## 5. Power electronics: T, A
- Diodes, thyristors, IGBTs and MOSFETs
- Rectifiers, inverters and DC-DC converters
- Variable frequency drives; UPS; PV inverters

## 6. Low-voltage installations: T, L, P
- Earthing systems (TN-S, TN-C-S, TT, IT)
- Protection against electric shock: protective earthing, TN ("ipus"), RCDs, separation transformers, double insulation, SELV/PELV, equipotential bonding ⟶ *protect-edu*
- Overcurrent protection: MCBs, fuses, breakers; trip curves
- Cable selection: ampacity, installation methods, correction factors, voltage drop, short-circuit withstand
- Distribution boards: design and labeling
- Lighting installations; special locations
- Inspection and testing: insulation resistance, loop impedance, RCD tests

## 7. Regulations and standards: L, P
- Structure of Israeli electricity law and regulations (concepts and navigation)
- Licensing classes and what each license permits
- Key regulations by topic (earthing, protection, wiring, special installations), always linking to the official text
- International standards context (IEC 60364 and others)

## 8. Medium and high voltage: T, A, P
- MV switchgear: functional units, interlocks, switching procedures ⟶ *hv-edu*
- Substations and transformers; protection relays
- Safety procedures, permits and grounding for work

## 9. Power-system analysis: A, P
- Per-unit system
- Load flow
- Symmetrical components; short-circuit calculations
- Protection coordination and selectivity
- Stability (intro)
- Grid integration of renewables; storage; microgrids ⟶ *Blue Grid*

## 10. Specialized niches: P
- Generators and backup power ⟶ *genset-edu*
- Airfield Ground Lighting (AGL) and series circuits ⟶ *agl-edu*
- PV systems; EV charging; data centers; hospitals; hazardous areas
- Energy efficiency and power quality

## Learning-path rules
- Every lesson declares its **prerequisites**: a topic graph that powers "learn this first" recommendations.
- Every module ends with a **quiz** drawn from the question bank, using the same topic tags.
- Licensing-track lessons link to the matching regulation sections and to licensing-simulator question sets.
